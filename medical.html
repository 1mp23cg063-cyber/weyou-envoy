Frequently Answered Questions

Healthcare & Hospital Communication AI.

Direct answers to how enVoy integrates with hospital workflows, complies with regulations, and impacts patient outcomes.

What is AI patient engagement software?+

AI patient engagement software is an intelligent digital communication platform that connects patients with healthcare providers across channels like WhatsApp, SMS, web chat, and voice. It automates administrative tasks including inquiry triage, appointment scheduling, pre-visit preparation, queue updates, billing queries, and post-discharge follow-ups, reducing staff workload while ensuring patients receive timely care guidance 24/7.

How does WhatsApp automation work for hospitals?+

Hospitals deploy enVoy on their official verified WhatsApp Business number. When a patient sends a message, enVoy's AI understands the patient's intent, queries the hospital's live appointment calendar or HMS in real time, offers available doctor slots, and confirms bookings instantly. Throughout the care cycle, enVoy sends timely reminders, queue alerts, and digital lab reports directly in the patient's WhatsApp thread.

How can hospitals reduce missed appointments (no-shows)?+

Hospitals reduce missed appointments (no-shows) by sending automated, contextual reminders via WhatsApp 24 hours and 2 hours prior to scheduled visits. enVoy allows patients to confirm, reschedule, or cancel with a single tap. Rescheduled slots are immediately released back into the live OPD queue, preserving hospital clinical capacity.

Can AI automate hospital appointment enquiries?+

Yes. enVoy understands patient symptoms, maps them to the appropriate medical department or specialist (e.g., Cardiology, Dermatology, Orthopedics), checks real-time doctor OPD schedules, and coordinates booking without putting patients on long phone holds.

Can WhatsApp AI integrate with hospital management systems (HMS/EMR)?+

Yes. enVoy connects securely to existing HMS, HIS, and EMR databases via RESTful APIs, HL7, or secure database connectors. It reads doctor schedules and writes confirmed bookings, patient contact details, and follow-up notes directly back into the hospital's existing system of record. It operates as an operational layer and does not replace the hospital's HMS or clinical decision-making systems.

How can hospitals automate post-discharge follow-ups?+

After a patient is discharged, enVoy triggers automated, protocol-driven check-ins at predetermined intervals (such as Day 3 and Day 7). The AI asks structured recovery questions regarding medication adherence, pain levels, and wound healing. If a patient reports concerning symptoms or high pain scores, enVoy instantly flags the issue and alerts the hospital nursing team or attending physician.

Is AI patient communication secure and compliant?+

Yes. enVoy adheres to India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023), ISO/IEC 27001 information security principles, and HIPAA-grade encryption standards. All patient conversations and health data are encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256 encryption. Patient consent is collected explicitly, and role-based access controls prevent unauthorized access.

How long does enVoy take to implement in a hospital?+

enVoy is designed for rapid enterprise deployment and typically goes live within 7 business days from onboarding. The process involves ingesting the hospital's specialties, doctors, pricing, and FAQs, configuring the WhatsApp Business API number, testing calendar/HMS connectors, and training staff on the human handover dashboard.

What languages does enVoy support for patients?+

enVoy supports all major Indian and international languages. Out of the box, it provides fluent communication in English, Kannada, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, and Malayalam. It dynamically detects the language or script used by the patient and can switch languages mid-conversation seamlessly.

How is enVoy different from a regular healthcare chatbot?+

Traditional chatbots rely on rigid keyword-matching decision trees and button menus that frustrate patients when questions deviate from the script. In contrast, enVoy is an operational AI communication system powered by contextual natural language understanding, live bi-directional system synchronization (reading and writing to live calendars/HMS/CRM), and smart escalation to human staff when necessary.
