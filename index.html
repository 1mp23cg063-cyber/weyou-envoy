What people usually ask.

So, what exactly is enVoy?+

enVoy is the AI communication system enterprises give to their customers — WhatsApp, email, web chat, app and voice, connected to the CRM. A weYou X product. Chatbot? CRM? Call-centre replacement? Appointment manager? None of them on their own — all of them at once. enVoy is the engine behind every channel, reaching each customer through the best method for the moment.

How is enVoy different from ChatGPT?+

ChatGPT is a general-purpose AI. enVoy is trained on your business — your prices, your products, your common questions, your tone — and runs on the channels your customers already use. The AI is one part; the system around it does the work.

How quickly does enVoy go live?+

7 days from signup. We train enVoy on your prices, common questions, and tone — then connect it to your WhatsApp number, your team’s calendars, and your records.

Which industries is enVoy built for?+

Six right now — medical, schools & colleges, spas & wellness, real estate, resorts & hotels, and any inbound business with a meaningful ticket size.

What languages does enVoy speak?+

Any written language — Kannada, Hindi and English out of the box. enVoy switches language mid-chat, reading what the customer typed and replying in kind.

Does enVoy connect to my systems?+

Yes. enVoy connects to your records, your inventory, and your team’s calendars. It checks what’s available live — rooms, slots, stock, pricing — and writes every customer’s details back.
