Privacy Policy.

Effective Date: November 4, 2026 · WeYouX

1. Overview and Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how WeYouX (“WeYouX”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), operating the weYou enVoy brand (“enVoy”), collects, uses, and safeguards information when organizations and their patients or customers interact with our website (weyouenvoy.com) and our WhatsApp and omnichannel communication orchestration software.

2. Data Processing Roles and Compliance

We comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA 2023), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and applicable Indian data protection frameworks. In the context of hospital and clinical deployments:

  • Healthcare Provider as Data Fiduciary: The hospital, clinic, or enterprise client remains the primary Data Fiduciary determining the purpose and means of patient communication.
  • enVoy as Data Processor: enVoy operates strictly as a Data Processor, routing appointments, reminders, and patient inquiries on behalf of the healthcare institution without claiming ownership of patient medical data.

3. Categories of Information Processed

We process only data required to deliver patient communication and appointment management:

  • Contact Information: Patient name, phone number (WhatsApp MSISDN), and email address.
  • Scheduling Metadata: Department preference, consulting doctor, preferred consultation slot, and appointment confirmation tokens.
  • Business Inquiry Information: Name, work email, organization, and communication preferences submitted through our website contact forms.
  • Technical Metadata: IP address, browser type, and interaction timestamps captured for security, audit logging, and SSRF prevention.

4. Zero Sale and Model Training Prohibition

We enforce a strict confidentiality guarantee:

  • We never sell, lease, or monetize personal data, patient health information, or corporate inquiries.
  • Patient communication data is never used to train public third-party foundation models.

5. Data Security and Infrastructure Isolation

All data in transit is encrypted using modern TLS 1.3 cryptographic protocols. All communications across the WhatsApp Business API run on Meta-verified, end-to-end encrypted networks. Databases and lead stores operate with AES-256 encryption at rest and strict tenant-level row segregation.

6. Data Subject Rights (DPDPA 2023)

Under DPDPA 2023, data principals retain the right to access their information, request correction or erasure, withdraw communication consent, and nominate representatives. To exercise these rights, email our Grievance Redressal Officer at privacy@weyoux.com.

7. Corporate Structure

weYou enVoy is an independent product brand operated by parent company WeYouX (Bengaluru, Karnataka, India). Sibling brands in the WeYouX ecosystem include LegalIndia.ai and WeYou World, each operating with separate data environments and tenant pipelines.
